# Nib & Leaf

**A design philosophy for the ExcellentPDF mark (Classic Indigo, Inscribed Edition).**

Nib & Leaf holds that a great reading and writing tool unites two crafts: the quiet clarity of a printed page and the
precision of a well-balanced fountain pen. Nothing extra is admitted.

Space is held by a layered pair of pages: a cool periwinkle sheet resting slightly fanned behind a warm ivory hero leaf,
hinting at open tabs, notebooks and workspaces without crowding the silhouette. The front leaf carries a cleanly turned
top-right corner with a soft diagonal crease shadow and faint ruled lines.

On the page itself, at display sizes (`64 px` and above), Revelation 22:13 (NKJV)—*“¹³ I am the Alpha and the Omega,
the Beginning and the End, the First and the Last.” — (NKJV Revelation 22:13)*—is inscribed in Palatino Italic with a
true superscript verse numeral over visible stationery rules, giving the sheet the intimate presence of a real
manuscript while simplifying cleanly to crisp bars at `24–48 px` and pure silhouette geometry at `16–20 px`.

Poised diagonally across the lower right and breaking past the page edge is a sculpted fountain-pen nib: two faceted
halves in royal indigo, a dual-ring satin brass ferrule, a gold breather hole and ink slit stopping cleanly inside the
pointed tip, and a delicate iridium glint meeting a fluid, pressure-tapered indigo ink flourish. Every size from 16 to
1024 pixels is rendered analytically for its own pixel grid so it holds crisp contrast on both light and dark taskbars.
